Privacy isn't a feature. It's the foundation.

CTRAI is built around a single principle: your financial data belongs to you, and only you. Here's exactly what that means in practice.

  • ✓
    No account, no login You install the app and you're in. No emails, no phone numbers, no sign-up forms.
  • ✓
    No cloud, no servers Transactions, accounts, budgets and goals live exclusively in the iOS sandbox on your device.
  • ✓
    No analytics, no ads Zero third-party trackers. No advertising SDKs. No behavior profiling.

Frequently asked questions

Quick clarity on how CTRAI handles your data and money.

Do I need to create an account?

No. CTRAI has no account system. Install the app and start tracking immediately — there is nothing to register for.

Where is my financial data stored?

All transactions, accounts, budgets and goals are stored locally in the app's private sandbox on your iPhone. They never leave your device unless you explicitly export them.

Does CTRAI connect to my bank?

No. CTRAI does not connect to bank accounts, brokerage APIs or any third-party financial services. Every entry is logged by you, by hand or by receipt photo.

Can I back up my data?

Your data is included in your standard iOS device backups (iCloud or iTunes/Finder), protected by the same encryption as the rest of your device. CTRAI itself does not maintain its own cloud backup.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings → Data → Reset to clear all CTRAI data. Uninstalling the app also removes everything stored by CTRAI from the device.

Which iOS versions work with CTRAI?

CTRAI requires iOS 16 or later and runs on iPhone in both light and dark appearance, with a fully designed dark experience out of the box.

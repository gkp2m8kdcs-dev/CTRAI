Privacy Policy

Last updated:

CTRAI is designed so that your financial data never leaves your iPhone. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what data the app handles, where it lives, and who can see it.

TL;DR. CTRAI does not require sign-up, does not collect personal data, does not use third-party trackers, and does not sync to any cloud. All transactions, accounts, budgets, goals and settings are stored exclusively on your device.

1. Who we are

CTRAI ("the App", "we", "us") is a personal finance application distributed under the name "CTRAI". For any privacy-related inquiry, you can reach us at Mefepzavap@icloud.com.

2. Information stored locally on your device

The following information is created and stored entirely on your iPhone, in the app's private sandbox. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party:

  • Display name and preferences you set in the app.
  • Accounts you create (name, institution, last-four digits, balance, color).
  • Transactions you log (amount, date, category, merchant, note, location, tags, attached receipt images).
  • Categories, budgets, savings goals and progress data.
  • Settings such as currency, monthly reset day, haptic feedback, and privacy blur.

Receipt images you attach are stored inside the app's sandbox using iOS file protection. They are not uploaded anywhere.

3. Permissions requested by the app

CTRAI requests the following iOS permissions solely to provide in-app features. You can deny any of them and the app will continue to work with reduced functionality.

  • Camera — to capture receipt photos you attach to a transaction. Photos remain in the app sandbox unless you export them.
  • Photo Library (read) — to attach existing images from your library to a transaction.
  • Photo Library (write) — to save exported report cards to your library when you choose to export them.

These permissions are requested only when you use the related feature, and the app does not access photos outside of those moments.

4. Backups

CTRAI does not maintain its own backup server. The app's local data is included in your standard iOS device backups (iCloud or iTunes/Finder) when device backup is enabled, where it benefits from the same encryption as the rest of your device. You can disable iOS backups for CTRAI at any time from the iOS Settings app.

5. Analytics, tracking and advertising

CTRAI does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, attribution frameworks, fingerprinting libraries, or behavioral trackers. The app makes no automated outbound requests for the purpose of profiling or advertising.

6. Children

CTRAI is intended for users aged 16 and older (or older if required by local law). The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children, since it does not collect personal data from anyone.

7. Your rights and choices

Because all data is local, the most direct way to exercise your rights is to act on the data on your device:

  • Access — open the app; all your data is there.
  • Export — use the export/share feature inside the app where available.
  • Delete — open Settings → Data → Reset, or delete the app to remove everything CTRAI stored on the device.

If you wish, you may also contact us at Mefepzavap@icloud.com with any privacy request.

8. International users

CTRAI does not transfer personal data across borders because it does not transfer personal data at all. Where local law (such as the GDPR or UK GDPR) grants you specific rights, CTRAI's local-only design means those rights are inherently satisfied by the architecture itself.

9. Changes to this policy

If we ever change this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you through the App Store listing. Because the app stores no account information, we cannot contact you directly inside the app.

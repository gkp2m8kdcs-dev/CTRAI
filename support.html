Quick troubleshooting

Where is my data stored?

Everything you enter lives on your iPhone, in the app's private sandbox. CTRAI does not upload or sync anything to a server, so there is nothing to "log in" to.

How do I back up my data?

Your data is included in your normal iOS device backups (iCloud or computer). You can disable backups for CTRAI from iOS Settings if you prefer to keep it device-only.

How do I clear all my data?

Open CTRAI → Settings → Data → Reset to Sample Data, or simply uninstall the app. Either path removes everything CTRAI has on the device.

Camera or photos not working?

Open iOS Settings → CTRAI and confirm Camera and Photos permissions are enabled. If a permission was denied, the in-app feature will guide you back to the toggle.

Frequently asked questions

Which devices and iOS versions are supported?

CTRAI runs on iPhone with iOS 16 or later. We test on the latest two major iOS releases.

Do I need an account to use CTRAI?

No. CTRAI is fully usable without any account, login, or email. Install the app and you're in.

Does CTRAI connect to my bank or broker?

No. CTRAI does not connect to any financial institution. You log transactions manually or by snapping a receipt.

Why is my currency symbol wrong?

Open Settings → Currency and pick the right currency. The list includes the symbol used by that currency (e.g. ¥, €, £, $, $).

How do I report a bug?

Email Mefepzavap@icloud.com with your iOS version, iPhone model, and a short description. Screenshots help us a lot.

Can I request a feature?

Absolutely. Send feature requests to Mefepzavap@icloud.com. We read every message.
